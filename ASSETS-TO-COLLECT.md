# Poseidon: assets for the finished website

The concept works now with a supplied textured 3D trident, illustrative artwork and sample projects. Nothing here blocks reviewing the scroll experience.

## Most useful next asset: a hero sculpture

- Look for a **Poseidon bust** or a **sculptural trident**, preferably with a calm, powerful silhouette rather than a game-character look.
- Supply a **GLB (.glb)** file with its materials included. A photograph or video cannot replace a rotatable 3D model.
- Prefer a model under **5 MB** and roughly **50,000 triangles or fewer**. We can assess and optimise a larger one if necessary.
- Colours can be adapted to aged bronze, ivory stone and deep sea green. A neutral model is fine.
- Keep the original asset page and licence. It must allow commercial website use, including supplying the model to visitors’ browsers.
- Useful search phrases: “Poseidon bust GLB”, “bronze trident low poly GLB”, “Greek sculpture game ready”. Share a candidate link before buying anything.

## Brand work for the artwork ring

- **6–10 distinct images**: packaging, posters, stationery, identity boards, signage or editorial campaign images.
- JPG, PNG or WebP; ideally at least **1,600 pixels on the long edge**. A mix of portrait and landscape compositions works well.
- Use real Poseidon projects when available. Keep permission to display each project. Current concept work is labelled as illustrative.
- For each project: name, a short description, and what Poseidon did.

## Final studio identity

- Logo in SVG, or a large transparent PNG.
- Any agreed colours and typefaces, including font licences if supplied.
- Real contact details and approved testimonials before launch.
